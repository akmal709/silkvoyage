When to Visit

Seasonal
Travel Guide

Uzbekistan rewards every season. Here's what awaits you at each time of year.

🌸
Spring
March — May

The most magical season. Flowers blanket the steppe, perfect temperatures (15–25°C), and Navruz New Year celebrations fill every city.

⭐ Best Season
☀️
Summer
June — August

Hot and golden (35–40°C), but perfect for early-morning monument visits. Ripe melons, sweet grapes, and long amber evenings.

🍉 Harvest Season
🍂
Autumn
September — November

Golden light on turquoise domes. Pomegranate season, harvest festivals, and perfect 20–28°C temperatures for exploring.

⭐ Best Season
❄️
Winter
December — February

Few tourists, snow-dusted monuments of extraordinary beauty, and unique photography. Ski season in Chimgan mountains.
